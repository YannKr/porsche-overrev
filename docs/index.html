Porsche over-rev reader

See how often your engine went past the rev limit, and when. Made for the 991.2 GT3. It only reads.

  • An OBDLink adapter, such as the MX+
  • Ignition on, engine off

Pick a way

On your phone

The OBDLink app. No code.

  1. Download the PID file on the phone.
  2. In the app: Settings › User‑Defined PIDs › ⋮ › Import. It replaces PIDs you added yourself.
  3. Open PID Values.

The file only reads. The app itself can also clear codes.

✓ Import tested on an iPhone

In this browser

Chrome or Edge on a computer. Nothing to install.

Or with a Raspberry Pi or another Linux computer:

Once, in a terminal

Installs overrev and reads the car.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/YannKr/porsche-overrev/main/get.sh | sh
New: not yet tried with the car

Every time the Pi starts

A Pi in the car reads at each start and posts to Discord or ntfy.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/YannKr/porsche-overrev/main/get.sh | sh -s -- --auto
New: not yet tried with the car

Read in this browser

  1. Pair the OBDLink with this computer, or plug in a USB one.
  2. Plug it into the car, under the dashboard.
  3. Ignition on, engine off. Close the OBDLink app on your phone.

Then choose the OBDLink in the list.

    Only reads: it never changes anything in the car. This page sends your results nowhere.

    Tested on one car: a US 991.2 GT3 (DME 991.618.692.17, software 3671). Other DMEs get a note.
